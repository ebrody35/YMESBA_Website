01 — About Us

A pipeline into the industry, built by students who love it.

Yale College's Media, Entertainment, and Sports Business Association is a student organization that empowers future leaders of the entertainment and sports industries through our guest speaker series, group projects, networking opportunities, information sessions, and other impactful experiences.

Founded in 2024 by Tommy Gannon, James Gumina, and Ava Seymour, Class of 2027
